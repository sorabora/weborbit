Welcome to the Weborbit Forum! Please read these before posting.

1. You must be 13 or older to have an account here. No exceptions.

2. Be respectful. No insults, harassment, or personal attacks toward other members. Disagree with ideas, not people. Casual profanity is fine, just don't aim it at people.

3. No slurs, hate speech, or NSFW content.

4. Stay on topic. Posts should relate to Weborbit: gameplay, rockets, mods, bug reports, suggestions, or community chat.

5. No spam. Don't post the same thing repeatedly, advertise unrelated stuff, or bump threads with empty replies.

6. Don't mess with the site. Weborbit is singleplayer, so cheat, mod, and glitch your rockets into orbit however you like and share how you did it. The forum itself is different: don't try to break it, bypass permissions, post things meant to run code in other people's browsers, or impersonate other users. If you find a security hole in the site, tell an admin privately instead of using it.

7. Protect privacy. Never share anyone's personal information (real name, address, accounts), yours or someone else's.

8. One account per person. Don't make alt accounts to dodge bans or pretend to be someone else.

9. Respect the moderators. Mods and admins may edit or delete posts that break these rules. If you think a decision was wrong, message a mod politely instead of arguing in threads.

Breaking these rules may get your post deleted, and repeat or serious offenses may get your account banned.

Have fun, be kind, and happy launching!
